One house, all to yourself

Camp Olea is a single safari house rather than a lodge with rooms in it. Your party has all five en-suite bedrooms, the kitchen, the lounge, the deck, the pool and the boma for the length of your stay, with no other guests arriving and no shared spaces to negotiate.

Staff live off the main house and are there when you want them — meals, drives, drinks, housekeeping — and out of sight when you don't.
